01Categories of personal data collected

Apple Data Safety: “Data Collected.”

A. Account and sign-in data

If you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address (for account creation, email sign-in, and verification)
  • Password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form, never in plain text)
  • Display name (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional, and only an image you choose to upload)

You may also use Sign in with Apple. In that case Apple shares your name and either your email address or a private Apple relay email, based on the choice you make. We use this only to create and secure your account. The same sign-in links your phone and your Apple Watch to the same account.

Purpose: Account creation, sign-in, security, and syncing your activity across your devices
Apple classification: Contact Info, User Content
Linked to user: Yes

B. Activity you create in the App

When you are signed in, we store the actions you take so your experience stays in sync across your phone and Apple Watch:

  • Reactions to stories (such as like, love, pray, and amen)
  • Saved or bookmarked articles
  • Feedback you give on a story’s editorial score
  • Comments or messages you choose to post

This activity is stored to provide these features to you. It is not used to build an advertising or behavioral profile.

Purpose: Deliver and sync your saved items, reactions, and feedback
Apple classification: User Content
Linked to user: Yes

C. News tips you submit

If you submit a news tip, you may include a description, a general location (such as a town), and, only if you choose, your name and a contact email or phone number so an editor can follow up. You may submit a tip anonymously, in which case no name or contact details are sent.

Purpose: Review and follow up on reader news tips
Apple classification: Contact Info, User Content
Linked to user: Only if you provide contact details

D. Other information you provide

  • Communications you send to us (feedback, inquiries, support requests)

E. Data collected automatically

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Usage data (article views, session duration)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics
  • IP address (used transiently for security and analytics)
  • Push notification token (only if you enable notifications, so we can deliver them)

Purpose: App functionality, analytics, security, and notifications
Apple classification: Identifiers, Usage Data, Diagnostics
Linked to user: Usage and diagnostics are aggregated; a push token is linked only to deliver your notifications

F. Optional device access

  • Location: If you allow it, we use your device location only to show local weather in the App. It is used on your device for that purpose and is not stored or linked to your identity. You can decline and still use everything.
  • Photos: We access your photo library only when you choose a profile picture. Only the image you select is uploaded, as your avatar. We do not otherwise access, scan, or upload your photos.

G. Data we do not collect

Dot 1 News does not collect:

  • Your contacts
  • Continuous or background location tracking
  • Audio or video recordings
  • Financial or payment information
  • Biometric identifiers
  • Health data
  • Other special-category or sensitive personal identifiers

03Legal basis for processing (GDPR, Article 6)

Dot 1 News processes personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): when you voluntarily provide information or enable an optional feature
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): to provide the account features you ask for, such as saving articles and syncing reactions
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): app security, performance, and editorial integrity
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): compliance with applicable laws

We do not process personal data for automated decision-making or profiling under Article 22.

04Use of information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Operate and maintain the App
  • Provide account features and sync your saved items, reactions, and feedback across your devices
  • Send push notifications you have enabled
  • Review and follow up on news tips you submit
  • Improve stability and performance
  • Respond to user communications
  • Protect platform integrity
  • Fulfill legal and regulatory obligations

Dot 1 News does not sell or monetize personal data.

05Editorial scoring & non-profiling notice

Dot 1 News applies a proprietary Four-Lens Content Scoring Model:

  • Biblical Alignment
  • Prophetic Significance
  • Source Credibility
  • Humanities Impact

These scores:

  • Are editorial judgments, not algorithmic profiling
  • Are not personalized
  • Do not rely on user behavior or identity
  • Do not constitute automated decision-making under GDPR

06Data sharing & disclosure

We may share limited data with:

  • Infrastructure and hosting providers
  • Security and analytics vendors (privacy-compliant)
  • Apple, when you use Sign in with Apple, and Apple’s push notification service when you enable notifications

All third parties are contractually bound to:

  • Use data only as instructed
  • Maintain confidentiality
  • Comply with applicable data protection laws

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

07Data retention

Personal data is retained:

  • Or as required by law

Users may request deletion at any time.
